# WO-003 — Ingestion: job runner and first marketplace source

Owner: data-ai-engineer · Opened 2026-11-15
Status: DONE-PENDING-QA (recalls); marketplace sources not started · Size L

## Why
Requirement F9 and milestone M1: a scraping job fills `source_records` and creates products without typing.

## Scope
`modules/ingestion`: `POST /ingest`, `GET /jobs/{id}`, the `ingest` queue handler implementing the seven steps
in the technical documentation section 6.2 (queue, fetch, keep raw copy with SHA-256, normalise, match or
create, media hand-off, publish `ProductIngested`). One source client, replaced wholesale in tests.

## Blocked on human
SerpApi and ScraperAPI keys, and Telcron's confirmation of the legal position for each source (risk 4).

## Log
- 2026-11-21: the human chose free, key-free sources over SerpApi/ScraperAPI. Built the first source: the US CPSC
  recall feed (SaferProducts.gov, public). `modules/ingestion` (`POST /ingest`, `GET /jobs`, `GET /jobs/{id}` for
  admins; `GET /recalls` public), the `ingest` queue handler, and a nightly sync at 03:00 UTC of the last 14 days.
  Each record's raw payload is kept once by SHA-256 (`source_records`); an unchanged record stops there, a changed
  one replaces the stored recall's products and identifiers. Brands come from the notice title, model numbers
  from its text (the feed's own Model and UPC fields were empty in all 45 records of October 2026). A recall that
  names a registry product's model, GTIN or (same brand, similar name) triggers its re-screen.
  Web: `/recalls` (public, searchable) and `/data-sources` (admin: start a run, follow it).
- Verified against the live feed in Chrome on the local stack: 45 notices fetched and stored; search "heater"
  returns the two heater recalls. The pipeline's browser stack reads a recorded copy (`CPSC_FIXTURE`).
- Not done: marketplace product listings (SerpApi/ScraperAPI declined; no free equivalent with terms that allow it),
  openFDA, certificate sources, the media hand-off.
