# WO-013 — Sign-up codes by email on the live site (Level 1 = verified email)

Owner: platform-identity-engineer (Claude Code session) → security-auditor · Opened 2026-11-21
Status: DONE-PENDING-QA · Size M (corrected by WO-018, 2026-11-21: merged to `main`, live since `730f04d`/`d7a05b1` with Gmail SMTP; sign-up on the dev site still closed — WO-021)
Supersedes the email half of WO-005. Decision: ADR-006 (`docs/decisions/ADR-006-level1-email-only.md`).

## Why
People sign up on the dev site and no code ever arrives: nothing sends email or SMS. The human decided
(2026-11-21): send codes by email through Amazon SES, and let Level 1 need only a verified email until
an SMS route exists (SMS to India needs DLT registration).

## Out of scope
SMS / phone verification (needs an SMS provider and DLT registration), password reset, Google sign-in.

## Risks
- Without phone verification one person can hold several Level 1 accounts (ADR-006). Coin fraud
  holds and the daily cap still apply. Revisit before TelCoins have real value.
- The live database gets the new migration on the first deploy of this branch.
