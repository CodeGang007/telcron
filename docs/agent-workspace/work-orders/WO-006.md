# WO-006 — Safety-team queue: confirm or reject flags, resolve tasks and fraud holds

Owner: community-rewards-engineer + frontend-engineer · Opened 2026-11-15
Status: DONE-PENDING-QA · Size M

## Why
Requirement F7. Flags and Medium/High screenings create `review_tasks`, but nothing can act on them, so a
hazard flag can never be confirmed and its 25 coins can never be earned.

## Scope
`POST /review-tasks/{id}/resolve`; confirming a flag sets its status, awards `hazard_flag` coins, emits
`HazardFlagConfirmed` (already routed to `screening`); releasing or confirming a fraud hold moves frozen coins;
an admin page listing the queue. Needs CT-001 before the two owners start.

## Log
- 2026-11-21: built in one session, without a separate contract (one author for both sides).
  Backend: `modules/moderation` (`GET /review-tasks`, `POST /review-tasks/{id}/claim`, `.../resolve` with
  confirm | reject | dismiss); `POST /products/{id}/fact-reports`, `GET /hazard-dimensions`, `GET /contributions`.
  A confirmed flag pays `hazard_flag_confirmed`, raises a `community_flag_confirmed` risk event for wallet
  holders, and is cited as evidence by the re-screen. An accepted correction changes the product (or its
  specifications), pays `fact_verification` and triggers a re-screen. A fraud hold is released or confirmed
  through `coin_resolve_hold`. Staff cannot decide their own contribution. Every decision is audited.
  Web: hazard and correction forms on the product page, `/review-queue` for admins and moderators, a member's
  reports and corrections with the decision and the reviewer's note on the rewards page.
- Trust score changes are PROVISIONAL amounts (`backend/app/domain/trust.py`, ADR-004): +2 confirmed flag,
  -1 rejected flag, +1 accepted correction, -0.5 rejected correction. The document gives no amounts.
- Not done: content moderation and media tasks (no media yet, WO-007); no notification to the member when a
  decision is made, they see it on the rewards page.
- Gates: `uv run pytest -q` 31 passed (5 new in `tests/test_moderation.py`); ruff clean; `tsc` clean; web build ok;
  `npm run journey` 5 passed, including a flag filed by a member and confirmed by the admin in a second browser.
