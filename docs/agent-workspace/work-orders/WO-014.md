# WO-014 — Research every product; photos from four sides; one product however many barcodes

Owner: data-ai-engineer + catalogue-engineer (one Claude Code session) → security-auditor · Opened 2026-11-21
Status: DONE-PENDING-QA · Size L

## Why
The human: a new product showed "Not enough information yet" forever; nothing searched the internet,
photos were thrown away, and the same product under another barcode became a second product.

## What was built
- `backend/app/modules/research/`: `registries.py` (UK OPSS, openFDA food/drug/device; no keys),
  `web.py` (Tavily: the open web, then government regulators only; fallback the model's browser
  search), `service.py` (a run = an ingestion job with source `web_search`; CPSC notices for the
  brand imported as recalls; findings stored as `web_document` evidence through
  `product_traceability`; official recall findings imported as recalls; product re-screened;
  passport cache cleared), `router.py` (`GET/POST /products/{slug}/research`).
- Research starts for every new product (barcode or member confirm), and on request (Level 1),
  at most once a day per product and 15 runs a day in all (Tavily free plan: 1,000 searches/month).
- Hallucination controls, in code: a finding can only name a result it was given (by number);
  pages carrying instructions are dropped before the model reads them; a finding must be about
  this product (model's flag) and the page must name the brand; and most of its words must be on
  the page (`grounded`). The scorer prompt (hazard-v3) knows web evidence.
- Evals: `backend/evals/research_cases.json` (10 cases, 3 traps), `make eval-research`.
  Run 2026-11-21 after the code checks: precision 0.889, recall 1.0, kind agreement 0.875, traps
  2/3, injected pages removed 1, other-product findings removed 2, ungrounded 0. The two misses
  are judgement calls (a shop listing's "auto shut-off" kept as a safety note; an EU withdrawal
  called a recall); expectations were not changed to fit.
- Photos: up to 4 per identification, stored in the private media bucket (`MEDIA_STORE=s3`),
  read by Amazon Rekognition (text + labels; `IMAGE_READER=rekognition`), then the vision model
  with what was read. Confirmed photos become the product's pictures (`/api/v1/media/{id}` →
  5-minute presigned link). Labels stored as `media_annotations`.
- Same product, other barcode: same brand and model number, or same brand and the title's core
  words (no colours, sizes, pack counts) at least 75% alike; two different model numbers never
  match. The member can also pick "Yes, it is this one" (`existing` on confirm).
- Passport rule (provisional, needs the human's sign-off with ADR-004): a hazard total in the high
  band (≥ 60) no longer gets "verified"; it gets "needs_information".
- AWS (the human's yes, 2026-11-21): inline policy `telcron-dev-rekognition` on the backend role;
  `TAVILY_API_KEY` added to the app secret; the deploy writes MEDIA_STORE/MEDIA_BUCKET/IMAGE_READER.
