# DEF-010 — Demo passports carry a hazard component of 100 with no hazard assessment behind it

Found by qa-test-engineer (WO-017, part A) on 2026-11-22 · Owner: database-engineer · Severity: S3
Status: OPEN

## What happens
`db/seeds/dev/sample_data.sql` inserts version-1 passports for the three tier-2 demo products with
`hazard_component = 100` and no `hazard_assessment_id`. No hazard assessment, no dimension score and no
citation exists for any of them. The product page draws a full-width "Hazard" bar for that 100 beside the
words "Not yet scored" (`frontend/src/pages/Product.tsx:278`). The trust score includes it: northwind
reads 96 and acme 12, both computed with an uncited hazard component of 100.

## What should happen
The application's own rule (`backend/app/domain/passport.py:58-60`): a missing hazard total "drops out of the
mean instead of counting as zero or as a hundred". CLAUDE.md: "Every hazard score cites evidence that
exists." The demo passports should have `hazard_component = NULL` and trust scores computed without it,
the way `passport_rules.build(...)` would compute them. Either fix the seed, or have the seed screen the
products through the application.

## Repro
1. Build a database in the order `make app-db` uses (migrations, `001_core_reference.sql`, phases 2–3,
   `db/seeds/dev/sample_data.sql`).
2. `GET /api/v1/products/northwind-chillmate-31/passport`

## Evidence

These are fictional demo products, but they are what visitors to the dev site see first.
