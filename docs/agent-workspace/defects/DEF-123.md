# DEF-123 — A "certificate issue" passport hides its reported hazards: the verdict names only the certificate

Found by browser-qa-engineer on 2026-11-23 (WO-058 browser verification of DEF-113, local stack @ 80f18b2) · Owner: frontend-engineer (`frontend/src/components/Verdict.tsx`) · Severity: S4
Status: FIXED — browser QA PASS 2026-11-23 (045a149)

## What happens
`/products/contoso-fastcharge-65w` (badge `certification_issue`, hazard total 37.35 Medium, Health & Safety 100) shows
only "A certificate problem was found — One of its certificates has expired or been revoked. See the details below."
The `certification_issue` branch in `Verdict` comes before the hazard branches (`Verdict.tsx`, the ternary chain), so a
passport with a scored dimension of 100 says nothing about hazards in its headline. It does not say "checks passed"
(so DEF-113's first half holds), but WO-058's DEF-113 criterion also asks that such a passport "shows a warning that
hazards are reported and points to the scores".

## What should happen
With a certificate issue AND a Medium total or any dimension ≥ 60, the verdict names both (e.g. the certificate
sentence plus "Sources also describe a high hazard: open the hazard scores below"). The recall branches can stay first.

## Repro
1. Open `http://localhost:3200/products/contoso-fastcharge-65w`.
2. Read the passport's verdict line; compare with the Hazard tile "Medium hazard" and Health & Safety 100.

## Evidence
- `GET /api/v1/products/contoso-fastcharge-65w/passport`: `status_badge: certification_issue`, `hazard.total_score: 37.35`,
  a dimension at 100.0.
- Page text: "A certificate problem was found / One of its certificates has expired or been revoked. See the details below."
