# DEF-114 — A recalled product's passport offers "Where to buy" shop links

Found by browser-qa-engineer on 2026-11-23 (weeks 1-2 sweep, local stack @ 189a1c6, Worker running, COIN_CONFIRM_DELAY 15 s) · Owner: frontend-engineer · Severity: S3
Status: OPEN

## What happens
`/products/acme-powerblend-1000` has a recall verdict `match` and the red banner "This product is recalled … Stop
using it". At the top of the same page the "Where to buy" menu links to Amazon, Walmart, Best Buy, Target, eBay and
Google Shopping searches for "Acme Kitchen PowerBlend 1000 Blender PB-1000".

## What should happen
A product with a recall `match` should not be offered for purchase. Hide "Where to buy" (or replace it with the
recall remedy / safer alternatives) when a `match` exists; for a `near_match`, at least warn next to the links.

## Repro
1. Visitor or member, open `http://localhost:3200/products/acme-powerblend-1000`.
2. Open "Where to buy".

## Evidence
- read_page: `link "Amazon" href="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Acme%20Kitchen%20PowerBlend%201000%20Blender%20PB-1000"`
  (and five more) on the same page as the recall banner.
- Passport: `recall_matches[0].verdict = "match"`.
