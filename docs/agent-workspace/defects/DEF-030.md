# DEF-030 — Product page forgets a saved product on reload: "Save to wallet" shows, clicking it errors

Found by browser-qa-engineer on 2026-11-22 (WO-017 run 2, local stack @ 706b0f8) · Owner: frontend-engineer · Severity: S3
Status: FIXED-PENDING-QA

## What happens
After saving a product, the button reads "In your wallet". After a reload (or on any later visit) the same
page shows "Save to wallet" again. Clicking it sends `POST /wallet/items` → `409 already_in_wallet`, shows the
toast "This product is already in your wallet." and the button stays "Save to wallet".

## What should happen
The page knows the product is in the member's wallet (from the wallet list, or a field on the product read)
and shows "In your wallet" on every visit; a 409 `already_in_wallet` at least flips the button.

## Repro
1. Sign in as a Level 1 member. Open `/products/contoso-fastcharge-65w`, click "Save to wallet" → "In your wallet".
2. Reload the page → button reads "Save to wallet". `/wallet` lists the product.
3. Click "Save to wallet" → toast "This product is already in your wallet.", button unchanged.

## Evidence
- `frontend/src/pages/Product.tsx:196` `const [saved, setSaved] = useState(false);` is local state, never
  initialised from the wallet; `:216-224` `save()` only sets it on success.
- Network: `POST /api/v1/wallet/items` 201 (first), later 409.
- Phone and desktop both affected (same component).

## Fix
frontend-engineer, 2026-11-22, on `week-02` @ 3e99ad4 (uncommitted).
The product page reads `GET /wallet/items` for a Level 1 member and shows "In your wallet" when the product is
there, on every visit; a 409 `already_in_wallet` also flips the button. `frontend/src/pages/Product.tsx`.
Gate: `npx tsc --noEmit` 0 errors; `npm run build` built. Browser walk not run (stack was not up).
