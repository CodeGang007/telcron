# DEF-116 — The review form pre-selects 5 stars, and the stars do not say which is selected

Found by browser-qa-engineer on 2026-11-23 (weeks 1-2 sweep, local stack @ 189a1c6, Worker running, COIN_CONFIRM_DELAY 15 s) · Owner: frontend-engineer · Severity: S3
Status: OPEN

## What happens
The "Write a review" form opens with all five stars filled: `frontend/src/components/Reviews.tsx:121`
`useState(5)`. A member who types a complaint and presses "Post review" publishes a 5-star review. The local data
shows exactly that: Northwind has several 5-star reviews reading "The compressor started to overheat and there was
smoke and a burning smell after a week", and the desk lamp a 5-star "the head gets very warm". Ratings feed the
evidence the scorer reads ("2/5 …" excerpts), so a default skews passports too.
Accessibility: the five rating buttons carry only `aria-label="n out of 5"`, no `aria-pressed`/radio state, so a
screen-reader user cannot tell the current rating; they are 28 px tall at 390 px (below a finger-sized target). The
read-only rating on each review is a `<span aria-label="2 out of 5">` with no role, which most screen readers ignore
(they read five star glyphs).

## What should happen
No rating is selected until the member picks one; "Post review" asks for a rating if none is chosen. The rating
control is a radio group (or buttons with `aria-pressed`) at least 40 px tall on phones; the displayed rating has
`role="img"` with its label.

## Repro
1. Sign in as a Level 1 member, open `/products/acme-powerblend-1000` → Reviews → "Review".
2. All five stars are gold before any click; inspect the buttons (no pressed/checked state).

## Evidence
- DOM: five buttons, class `fill-gold text-gold`, `aria-pressed=null`, `aria-checked=null`.
- 390 px measurement: "1 out of 5 … 5 out of 5" 28 px tall.
- `/products/northwind-chillmate-31` reviews: five-star "smoke and a burning smell".
