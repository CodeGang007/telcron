# DEF-037 — Home page shows an invented passport for a real catalogue product

Found by browser-qa-engineer on 2026-11-22 (WO-017 run 2, local stack @ 706b0f8) · Owner: frontend-engineer · Severity: S4
Status: FIXED-PENDING-QA

## What happens
The desktop hero card reads "Product passport · v3 · Verified · Northwind ChillMate Mini Fridge · trust 92 ·
Regulatory 6 · Health & safety 18 · Customer issues 31". Every figure is hard-coded. The product exists in the
catalogue, and its real passport today is version 5+ with trust 87 and Health & Safety 60. A visitor who searches
for it sees different numbers from the ones on the home page.

## What should happen
The illustration either uses a clearly fictional product marked "Example", or is bound to the real passport.
The rule is "the passport is honest" (`telcron-user-flows`), and a mock that looks like a real passport for a real
product breaks it.

## Repro
Open `/` at desktop width (the card is `hidden lg:block`); compare with `/products/northwind-chillmate-31`.

## Evidence
- `frontend/src/pages/Home.tsx:45-62` (`v3`, `badge="verified"`, `TrustRing score={92}`, `[['Regulatory', 6], ['Health & safety', 18], ['Customer issues', 31]]`).
- `GET /api/v1/products/northwind-chillmate-31/passport` this run: trust 87, version ≥ 5.

## Fix
frontend-engineer, 2026-11-22, on `week-02` @ 3e99ad4 (uncommitted).
The hero card is `frontend/src/components/PassportPreview.tsx`: it takes the first recent product with a trust
score (non-demo first), reads its current passport and shows only that passport's version, badge, trust ring, recall
count, hazard label and up to three scored dimensions, linked to the product. With no screened product it draws
nothing (a numberless placeholder while loading). The floating chips state rules, not product figures.
Gate: `npx tsc --noEmit` 0 errors; `npm run build` built. Browser walk not run (stack was not up).
