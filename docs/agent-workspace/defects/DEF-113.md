# DEF-113 — A Medium-hazard passport with Health & Safety 100 says "checks passed, nothing shows a serious hazard"

Found by browser-qa-engineer on 2026-11-23 (weeks 1-2 sweep, local stack @ 189a1c6, Worker running, COIN_CONFIRM_DELAY 15 s) · Owner: frontend-engineer (verdict copy) with data-ai-engineer (badge rule) · Severity: S2
Status: OPEN

## What happens
`/products/northwind-chillmate-31` (badge `verified`, trust 81, hazard total in the Medium band) opens with the green
verdict "No recall found, checks passed — No recall notice matches it, and nothing we found shows a serious
hazard. We keep checking." The same page scores **Health & Safety 100** ("9 of 12 evidence items mention this
hazard": owner reviews describing overheating, smoke and a burning smell) and the summary tile says "Medium hazard".
The verdict only turns to a warning when the weighted total is 60+ (`frontend/src/pages/Product.tsx:24`,
`high = total_score >= 60`); a single dimension at 100 never changes it. The "No recall found" line also carries no
as-of date (business rule §4: "no match found" is true as of the screening's corpus date).

## What should happen
A passport never tells a visitor "nothing shows a serious hazard" while it shows a maxed-out dimension backed by
evidence. Medium band (30–59) is "safety-team queue", not "checks passed" (business rules §3). Either the verdict
reflects the worst dimension / the band, or the badge rule stops issuing `verified` + "passed" for Medium. The
recall line states the date it is true as of.

## Repro
1. Local stack, visitor. Open `http://localhost:3200/products/northwind-chillmate-31`.
2. Read the verdict banner, then the "Hazard scores" card (Health & Safety 100) and the Hazard tile (Medium).
