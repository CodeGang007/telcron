# DEF-101 — The review queue offers a moderator decisions on their own item, and its buttons are 38 px on a phone

Found by browser-qa-engineer on 2026-11-22 (WO-017 final browser gate, local stack @ 838beea) · Owner: frontend-engineer (the "whose item is this" field, if needed, from the moderation module's owner) · Severity: S4
Status: OPEN

## What happens
The rule itself holds (DEF-011 PASS): a moderator who opens their own hazard flag or their own fraud hold in
`/admin/review-queue` gets `403 own_item` for every decision, and the card shows "You cannot decide on your own
item. Leave it for a colleague." Nothing changes, and a colleague can still decide it.
But the card offers three live buttons ("Confirm hazard / Not a hazard / Dismiss", "Confirm fraud / Release
coins / Dismiss") that can only fail. The moderator learns it is their own item only after clicking, and each
click is a 403 in the console.
Separately, on a phone (390 px) the decision buttons are 38 px tall (`btn !px-4 !py-2`), under the 44 px used
for every other tap target in the app (wallet Remove, Helpful are 44 px).

## What should happen
A task about the viewer's own contribution or coins is marked "Your item: a colleague decides this", with no
decision buttons (or disabled ones). The task list needs to say so (e.g. a `mine` flag on each task, as reviews
already have for DEF-031), or the page compares the subject's member with `me`. The decision buttons are at
least 44 px tall on a phone.

## Repro
1. Two moderators (local: verified members with `role = 'moderator'`). Moderator 1 files a hazard flag on any
   product; give them a fraud hold (`fraud_holds` row, `coin_apply_hold`, a `fraud_hold` review task, as in
   `backend/tests/test_moderation.py::hold_coins`).
2. Moderator 1 opens `/admin/review-queue` at 390 px and clicks each button on both cards.
