# DEF-119 — The report gate sends a member to the store, which cannot sell that product's report

Found by browser-qa-engineer on 2026-11-23 (weeks 1-2 sweep, local stack @ 189a1c6, Worker running, COIN_CONFIRM_DELAY 15 s) · Owner: frontend-engineer (CT-002 consumer) · Severity: S4
Status: FIXED — QA PASS + browser PASS 2026-11-23 (755d5b2)

## What happens
A signed-in member on `/products/northwind-chillmate-31/report` (product not in their wallet) sees "Redeem it in the
coin store with your confirmed TelCoins" and "Open the coin store". The store's report item only offers products in
the member's wallet ("Save a product to your wallet to get its report."; the "For" list is the wallet). The
passport's link "Get the full report with TelCoins" also goes to `/rewards`. The member lands in the store with no
way to buy the report they came for and no hint that they must save the product first.

## What should happen
Either the gate says "Save this product to your wallet, then redeem its report" with a Save button, or the store
accepts the product the member came from (e.g. `/rewards?product=<slug>` pre-selected).

## Repro
1. As a Level 1 member with ≥50 confirmed coins and an empty wallet, open `/products/northwind-chillmate-31/report`.
2. "Open the coin store" → the report item says "Save a product to your wallet" and Redeem is disabled.

- `frontend/src/pages/Rewards.tsx:144,165-176` (product list = wallet items).
- Gate text as observed above. With the blender in the wallet the redemption worked (115 → 65 coins, report unlocked,
  still unlocked after removing it from the wallet, other products still locked): PASS for CT-002 itself.
