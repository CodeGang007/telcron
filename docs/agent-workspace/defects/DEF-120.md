## What happens

hazard_insufficient_data`). Each shows only a product name, a date, "Mark reviewed" and "Dismiss": no dimension, no
passport version, nothing to check. A new task is opened on every re-screen of the same product (Contoso ×5,
Northwind ×3, each e2e "Lumina" product ×1–2). Insufficient data is the honest, expected state for a dimension with
no evidence (business rules §3), so most of these have no decision to make. The two hazard reports and the
correction were easy to miss among them.

the producta member asked".

## What should happen

passport version, or no task at all where nothing is expected of a person. Run lines read "last 30 days · by hand".
