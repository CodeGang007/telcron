# DEF-118 — Rewards page copy contradicts D4 and itself (earn cards, levels, streak)

Found by browser-qa-engineer on 2026-11-23 (weeks 1-2 sweep, local stack @ 189a1c6, Worker running, COIN_CONFIRM_DELAY 15 s) · Owner: frontend-engineer (copy; earn-rule labels may live in reference data → database-engineer) · Severity: S4
Status: OPEN
Items 1–3 were observed by the human on the dev site's rewards page; all three reproduce locally. Items 4–6 found in
this sweep.

## What happens
1. Earn card "+100 Verify your email and phone". D4/ADR-006: Level 1 is the email code only; there is no phone check.
2. Levels list "Novice (you) · 0 coins earned", "Inspector · 200 coins earned …" reads as the member's own total; it is
   the threshold. A member with 105 coins sees "0 coins earned" next to "you".
3. Streak box "Sign-in streak 1 day · 6 more for double coins" vs earn card "+10 Sign in 7 days in a row". One says
   coins double after 7 days, the other a flat +10; business rules: 5/day, 10/day from day 7.
4. The reward level card says "Novice · Level 1": "Level 1" is already the verification level (TelcronID Level 1),
   so a Level 1 member reads two different "Level 1"s on one page.
5. "+15 Add a photo to a review": the review form has no photo field (WO-007, W4).
6. "+500 Verify your identity": Level 2 is not built (WO-010); nothing on the site lets a member do it.
Also, sign-up's optional phone placeholder is `+919876543210` (an Indian number) for a US market (D8).

## What should happen
Copy matches the rules: "+100 Verify your email"; thresholds read "from 200 coins"; streak copy agrees ("+5 a day,
+10 a day from day 7"); reward levels are not called "Level n"; unavailable earns are hidden or marked "coming soon";
a US-format phone placeholder.

1. Sign up locally (DEV_EXPOSE_CODES shows the code), verify, open `/rewards`.
2. Read "Ways to earn", the streak box, the level card and "Levels".
